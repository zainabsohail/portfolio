Motivation

I studied software engineering in Karachi, moved to Germany in 2021 to study AI, and have spent almost five years at Siemens Healthineers learning how *data turns into decisions.* What drives me is the point where *AI, digitalization and business meet:* taking a messy question, finding the evidence and shaping it into something a team can act on. Moving countries taught me to adapt fast and build from scratch. Now I want a role that *stretches me,* with new teams and problems I can't solve yet.

Mindset

How I work, in five lines.

1

I don't wait for a title to take ownership.

When something in my team needs doing, I pick it up. My manager watched me go from supporting tasks to owning deliverables and running cost-saving work on my own.

2

If a task repeats, I automate it.

I've automated more than 20 workflows with Power Automate, and colleagues now come to me when they need a technical solution.

3

Evidence should change a decision.

Numbers on a slide aren't the goal. When I present to management, I shape the findings so they can act on them.

4

A tool only works if people use it.

Training sessions, guides and clear communication are part of the build. That's how a Power App gets to 200+ users.

5

Ask questions, learn fast, deliver.

I don't expect to know everything on day one. I'd rather ask early and turn what I learn into results.
